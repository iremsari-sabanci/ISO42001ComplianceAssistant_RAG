# PR-AIS-001 — Model Validation Procedure

- **Procedure owner:** Artificial Intelligence Solutions Directorate
- **Status:** Not yet created - required by ISO 42001
- **Related Annex A controls:** A.6.2.4
- **RAG corpus layer:** Layer 2 — internal governance

## Purpose
Defines independent validation of AI models before deployment and periodically thereafter.

> **ACTION REQUIRED - PENDING ADDITION**
>
> This procedure does not yet contain the ISO/IEC 42001 provisions set out below. The addition must be drafted, approved and published by **Artificial Intelligence Solutions Directorate** as the procedure owner. Until it is published, AI systems assessed against A.6.2.4 may show gaps that system owners cannot remediate themselves.


## Required ISO 42001 additions (not yet published)
- Validation performed by a party not involved in development, reviewing development documentation, performance results, bias test results and explainability methods.
- Validation report content: executive summary with system identity, risk level and test outcomes; performance results against defined metrics; bias analysis; explainability methods used; and a reasoned go-live opinion.
- Periodic re-validation intervals set by risk level.

The provisions above have been proposed by the AI Governance Team. They are **not yet part of this procedure**. **Artificial Intelligence Solutions Directorate** is responsible for drafting, approving and publishing them; the AI Governance Team tracks closure but does not author other directorates' procedures.
