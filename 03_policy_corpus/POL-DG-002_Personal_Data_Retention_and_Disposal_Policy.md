# POL-DG-002 — Personal Data Retention and Disposal Policy

- **Procedure owner:** Data Governance Service (Data Management and Analytics Directorate)
- **Status:** Published - ISO 42001 addition pending
- **Related Annex A controls:** A.7.3, A.7.5
- **RAG corpus layer:** Layer 2 — internal governance

## Purpose
Defines retention periods and secure disposal for personal data.

## Scope and existing provisions
- Retention schedule by data category and secure destruction methods.
- Applies to archived records following AI system retirement.

> **ACTION REQUIRED - PENDING ADDITION**
>
> This procedure does not yet contain the ISO/IEC 42001 provisions set out below. The addition must be drafted, approved and published by **Data Governance Service (Data Management and Analytics Directorate)** as the procedure owner. Until it is published, AI systems assessed against A.7.3, A.7.5 may show gaps that system owners cannot remediate themselves.


## Required ISO 42001 additions (not yet published)
- Disposal scope extended beyond databases: training datasets, embeddings and vector indexes may themselves constitute personal data and a model may memorise it.
- How an erasure request propagates to training sets, indexes and, where technically feasible, the model; where not feasible the limitation is documented and risk-accepted.

The provisions above have been proposed by the AI Governance Team. They are **not yet part of this procedure**. **Data Governance Service (Data Management and Analytics Directorate)** is responsible for drafting, approving and publishing them; the AI Governance Team tracks closure but does not author other directorates' procedures.
