# PR-DG-005 — Transparency and Information Notices Standard

- **Procedure owner:** Data Governance Service (Data Management and Analytics Directorate)
- **Status:** Published - ISO 42001 addition pending
- **Related Annex A controls:** A.8.2, A.8.5
- **RAG corpus layer:** Layer 2 — internal governance

## Purpose
Defines the notices provided to customers and other interested parties.

## Scope and existing provisions
- Content, timing and channels for statutory information notices and explicit consent flows.

> **ACTION REQUIRED - PENDING ADDITION**
>
> This procedure does not yet contain the ISO/IEC 42001 provisions set out below. The addition must be drafted, approved and published by **Data Governance Service (Data Management and Analytics Directorate)** as the procedure owner. Until it is published, AI systems assessed against A.8.2, A.8.5 may show gaps that system owners cannot remediate themselves.


## Required ISO 42001 additions (not yet published)
- AI-specific disclosure where a system materially affects a person: that an AI system is involved, its purpose, the main factors influencing the output in understandable terms, and how to request human review.
- Disclosure that the user is interacting with an AI assistant rather than a person.
- Obligations to provide AI system information to regulators and other authorities on request.

The provisions above have been proposed by the AI Governance Team. They are **not yet part of this procedure**. **Data Governance Service (Data Management and Analytics Directorate)** is responsible for drafting, approving and publishing them; the AI Governance Team tracks closure but does not author other directorates' procedures.
