# POL-DG-001 — Personal Data Processing and Protection Policy

- **Procedure owner:** Data Governance Service (Data Management and Analytics Directorate)
- **Status:** Published - ISO 42001 addition pending
- **Related Annex A controls:** A.5.4, A.7.3, A.8.2
- **RAG corpus layer:** Layer 2 — internal governance

## Purpose
Governs the processing and protection of personal data across the Bank under KVKK.

## Scope and existing provisions
- Defines processing purposes, legal bases, data subject rights and the obligations of processors.
- Registration of processing activities and maintenance of the statutory registry entries.

> **ACTION REQUIRED - PENDING ADDITION**
>
> This procedure does not yet contain the ISO/IEC 42001 provisions set out below. The addition must be drafted, approved and published by **Data Governance Service (Data Management and Analytics Directorate)** as the procedure owner. Until it is published, AI systems assessed against A.5.4, A.7.3, A.8.2 may show gaps that system owners cannot remediate themselves.


## Required ISO 42001 additions (not yet published)
- AI added as a distinct processing purpose, with the legal basis for model training stated separately from service delivery.
- Rights against decisions produced solely by automated systems: a defined objection channel and human-review route.
- Special-category data (voiceprints, facial images, religion and blood-group fields) restricted to explicit-consent or explicit legal-basis cases and recorded in the impact assessment.

The provisions above have been proposed by the AI Governance Team. They are **not yet part of this procedure**. **Data Governance Service (Data Management and Analytics Directorate)** is responsible for drafting, approving and publishing them; the AI Governance Team tracks closure but does not author other directorates' procedures.
