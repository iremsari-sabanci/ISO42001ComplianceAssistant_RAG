# PR-DG-003 — Data Acquisition and Labelling Standard

- **Procedure owner:** Data Governance Service (Data Management and Analytics Directorate)
- **Status:** Not yet created - required by ISO 42001
- **Related Annex A controls:** A.7.3, A.7.6
- **RAG corpus layer:** Layer 2 — internal governance

## Purpose
Defines approved sources for AI data and the labelling process that produces training targets.

> **ACTION REQUIRED - PENDING ADDITION**
>
> This procedure does not yet contain the ISO/IEC 42001 provisions set out below. The addition must be drafted, approved and published by **Data Governance Service (Data Management and Analytics Directorate)** as the procedure owner. Until it is published, AI systems assessed against A.7.3, A.7.6 may show gaps that system owners cannot remediate themselves.


## Required ISO 42001 additions (not yet published)
- Approved sources for AI data and the licensing and intellectual-property position for external, purchased and web-derived data.
- Labelling standard: who labels, quality control of labels, inter-annotator agreement, and correction of label errors, since label quality is a direct driver of bias.
- Documentation of data preparation methods and the criteria for selecting them.

The provisions above have been proposed by the AI Governance Team. They are **not yet part of this procedure**. **Data Governance Service (Data Management and Analytics Directorate)** is responsible for drafting, approving and publishing them; the AI Governance Team tracks closure but does not author other directorates' procedures.
