## 1. Purpose
Defines how AI-specific risk is assessed and how impact assessments are conducted for systems that process personal data or make automated decisions.

## 2. Risk management
2.1 Every registered AI system is assigned a risk level (Low / Medium / High). The risk level determines monitoring cadence and the depth of ongoing risk treatment.

2.2 For Medium- and High-risk systems, a documented risk assessment identifies foreseeable harms, likelihood, and treatment measures. The assessment is revisited at each review cycle.

2.3 Risk treatment relies on evidenced data quality and documented data lineage for the system's inputs; where these are absent, the residual risk is recorded and escalated.

## 3. Impact assessment
3.1 An impact assessment is mandatory where a system processes personal data or produces automated decisions affecting individuals. It records the affected population, the potential effect on individuals, and mitigations.

3.2 The impact assessment is a prerequisite for the go/no-go decision for in-scope systems and is reviewed when the use case or data inputs change materially.

## 4. Scope note
4.1 This procedure covers risk and impact assessment. It does not, by itself, establish ongoing fairness or bias monitoring in operation; that is addressed by a separate responsible-use procedure where one exists.
