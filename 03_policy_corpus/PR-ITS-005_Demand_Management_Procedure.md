# PR-ITS-005 — Demand Management Procedure

- **Procedure owner:** IT Strategy and Governance Directorate
- **Status:** Published - ISO 42001 addition pending
- **Related Annex A controls:** A.5.2, A.6.2.2
- **RAG corpus layer:** Layer 2 — internal governance

## Purpose
Defines how demands are raised, classified, assessed and routed.

## Scope and existing provisions
- Demand intake, catalogue classification, process and data governance impact evaluation and channel impact assessment.

> **ACTION REQUIRED - PENDING ADDITION**
>
> This procedure does not yet contain the ISO/IEC 42001 provisions set out below. The addition must be drafted, approved and published by **IT Strategy and Governance Directorate** as the procedure owner. Until it is published, AI systems assessed against A.5.2, A.6.2.2 may show gaps that system owners cannot remediate themselves.


## Required ISO 42001 additions (not yet published)
- An AI data and system impact assessment field added to the impact evaluation step.
- A mandatory AI flag preferred over a separate AI catalogue: a separate catalogue fragments demand management and creates a route by which AI requests bypass it, whereas a flag keeps one intake route and still triggers Committee review.

The provisions above have been proposed by the AI Governance Team. They are **not yet part of this procedure**. **IT Strategy and Governance Directorate** is responsible for drafting, approving and publishing them; the AI Governance Team tracks closure but does not author other directorates' procedures.
