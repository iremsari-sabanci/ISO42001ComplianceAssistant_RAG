# PR-DG-001 — Data Quality Procedure

- **Procedure owner:** Data Governance Service (Data Management and Analytics Directorate)
- **Status:** Published - ISO 42001 addition pending
- **Related Annex A controls:** A.7.4
- **RAG corpus layer:** Layer 2 — internal governance

## Purpose
Defines how data quality is measured, monitored and remediated across data assets.

## Scope and existing provisions
- Data quality dimensions, measurement, issue logging and remediation ownership.
- Quality expectations agreed with source owners.

> **ACTION REQUIRED - PENDING ADDITION**
>
> This procedure does not yet contain the ISO/IEC 42001 provisions set out below. The addition must be drafted, approved and published by **Data Governance Service (Data Management and Analytics Directorate)** as the procedure owner. Until it is published, AI systems assessed against A.7.4 may show gaps that system owners cannot remediate themselves.


## Required ISO 42001 additions (not yet published)
- Data quality dimensions and acceptance thresholds for AI training data: accuracy, completeness, consistency, timeliness and representativeness of the operational population.
- Profiling required before each training and retraining cycle.
- Drift monitoring treated as continuous data quality, not only model monitoring.

The provisions above have been proposed by the AI Governance Team. They are **not yet part of this procedure**. **Data Governance Service (Data Management and Analytics Directorate)** is responsible for drafting, approving and publishing them; the AI Governance Team tracks closure but does not author other directorates' procedures.
