# PR-DG-002 — Metadata and Data Lineage Procedure

- **Procedure owner:** Data Governance Service (Data Management and Analytics Directorate)
- **Status:** Published - ISO 42001 addition pending
- **Related Annex A controls:** A.4.3, A.7.5
- **RAG corpus layer:** Layer 2 — internal governance

## Purpose
Defines registration of data assets, metadata capture and end-to-end lineage.

## Scope and existing provisions
- Data asset registration, business and technical metadata, data dictionary maintenance.
- Lineage from source systems through transformations to consuming reports.

> **ACTION REQUIRED - PENDING ADDITION**
>
> This procedure does not yet contain the ISO/IEC 42001 provisions set out below. The addition must be drafted, approved and published by **Data Governance Service (Data Management and Analytics Directorate)** as the procedure owner. Until it is published, AI systems assessed against A.4.3, A.7.5 may show gaps that system owners cannot remediate themselves.


## Required ISO 42001 additions (not yet published)
- Training, validation and test datasets registered as data assets with versioned snapshots so a decision can be reproduced.
- Lineage extended end to end: source, feature, model version, decision. Without model-version lineage the Bank cannot identify which model produced a challenged decision.

The provisions above have been proposed by the AI Governance Team. They are **not yet part of this procedure**. **Data Governance Service (Data Management and Analytics Directorate)** is responsible for drafting, approving and publishing them; the AI Governance Team tracks closure but does not author other directorates' procedures.
