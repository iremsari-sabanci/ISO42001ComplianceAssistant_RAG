# PR-ITS-001 — IT Risk Management Procedure

- **Procedure owner:** IT Strategy and Governance Directorate
- **Status:** Published - ISO 42001 addition pending
- **Related Annex A controls:** A.4.2, A.6.2.3
- **RAG corpus layer:** Layer 2 — internal governance

## Purpose
Defines identification, recording, treatment and monitoring of IT risks.

## Scope and existing provisions
- Threat and vulnerability catalogues, risk sources, asset and process registers, risk scoring and treatment.

> **ACTION REQUIRED - PENDING ADDITION**
>
> This procedure does not yet contain the ISO/IEC 42001 provisions set out below. The addition must be drafted, approved and published by **IT Strategy and Governance Directorate** as the procedure owner. Until it is published, AI systems assessed against A.4.2, A.6.2.3 may show gaps that system owners cannot remediate themselves.


## Required ISO 42001 additions (not yet published)
- AI-specific threats added: manipulation of the model through data poisoning, and faulty decision mechanisms triggered by model outputs.
- AI-specific vulnerabilities added: unexplainable decisions arising from black-box models, overfitting, drift, over-reliance on outputs (automation bias) and failure of human review to detect model error.
- New risk sources added: model performance monitoring, AI ethics assessments and model validation processes.
- Information asset definition extended so that AI models, algorithms, weights and the datasets on which they are trained are themselves information assets, together with prompts, system prompts, embeddings and vector indexes.

The provisions above have been proposed by the AI Governance Team. They are **not yet part of this procedure**. **IT Strategy and Governance Directorate** is responsible for drafting, approving and publishing them; the AI Governance Team tracks closure but does not author other directorates' procedures.
