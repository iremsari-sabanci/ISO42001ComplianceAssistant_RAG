# PR-AIGOV-002 — AI Governance Committee Procedure

- **Procedure owner:** AI Governance Team (Data Management and Analytics Directorate)
- **Status:** Published
- **Related Annex A controls:** A.2.2, A.3.2
- **RAG corpus layer:** Layer 2 — internal governance

## Purpose
Defines the constitution, membership, decision rights and records of the AI Governance Sub-Committee.

## Scope and existing provisions
- Members contribute opinions from their own domain: Analytics (methodology, explainability, performance metrics), Data Governance (transparency, KVKK compliance, impact assessment), Information Security (security monitoring, vulnerabilities), IT Infrastructure and Operations (resource consumption, observability, rollback), IT Strategy and Governance (enterprise architecture, project lifecycle).
- Regulatory Compliance and Legal Advisory join on the request of any member where a legal, ethical or internal-policy concern arises.
- Grants deployment authority following a positive decision.

> **ACTION REQUIRED - PENDING ADDITION**
>
> This procedure does not yet contain the ISO/IEC 42001 provisions set out below. The addition must be drafted, approved and published by **AI Governance Team (Data Management and Analytics Directorate)** as the procedure owner. Until it is published, AI systems assessed against A.2.2, A.3.2 may show gaps that system owners cannot remediate themselves.


## Required ISO 42001 additions (not yet published)
- Decision types with quorum: go / no-go, conditional approval, emergency shutdown, retirement, and residual-risk acceptance.
- Standing agenda item for not-applicable claims and control exemptions raised by system owners.
- Minutes to record the evidence reviewed for each decision, as the minutes form the audit trail for management review.

The provisions above have been proposed by the AI Governance Team. They are **not yet part of this procedure**. **AI Governance Team (Data Management and Analytics Directorate)** is responsible for drafting, approving and publishing them; the AI Governance Team tracks closure but does not author other directorates' procedures.
