## 1. Purpose
This policy establishes Meridian Financial Services' management direction and commitment to the responsible development, deployment, and use of artificial intelligence. It applies to all AI systems recorded in the AI system register, across their full life cycle.

## 2. Policy statements
2.1 Meridian commits to developing and operating AI systems in a manner that is lawful, fair, transparent, accountable, and subject to human oversight. This commitment is reviewed annually by the AI Governance Committee.

2.2 This AI policy is aligned with the institution's existing information security, personal data protection (KVKK), and risk management policies. Where an AI-specific requirement is not covered by an existing policy, a dedicated AI procedure is issued.

## 3. Organisational roles
3.1 The AI Governance Committee holds overall accountability for the AI management system. The Data Governance Department acts as secretariat.

3.2 Every AI system in the register has a named Business Owner (accountable for use-case outcomes) and Technical Owner (accountable for model artefacts and pipeline). Data stewardship is assigned where a formal steward exists.

3.3 Concerns regarding an AI system's behaviour may be raised to the Data Governance Department through the standard escalation channel.

## 4. AI system register
4.1 No AI system may enter production unless it is recorded in the AI system register with all mandatory inventory attributes populated.

4.2 The register is the authoritative source for governance scope and is reviewed at each Committee cycle.

## 5. Human oversight principle
5.1 AI systems that make or materially influence decisions affecting individuals must operate under defined human oversight. The nature and point of human intervention are documented per system in the relevant lifecycle record.
