# POL-AIGOV-001 — AI Policy

- **Procedure owner:** AI Governance Team (Data Management and Analytics Directorate)
- **Status:** Published
- **Related Annex A controls:** A.2.2, A.2.3, A.2.4
- **RAG corpus layer:** Layer 2 — internal governance

## Purpose
Establishes management direction and commitment for the responsible development, provision and use of AI systems across the Bank.

## Human oversight determination

- ISO/IEC 42001 does not prescribe a form of human oversight. It requires the organisation to
  determine whether oversight is necessary and, if so, what form it takes.
- The Bank makes that determination as follows. **Human oversight is required for every AI
  system that makes or materially drives an automated decision**, and the acceptable forms are
  human-in-the-loop (a person reviews or approves each output before it takes effect) or
  human-on-the-loop (a person monitors outputs and can intervene, override or halt the system).
- For systems that do not make automated decisions, oversight is not mandatory. Human-on-the-loop
  review may still be appropriate and is recorded where it exists.
- The determination for an individual system is made by the AI Governance Team and recorded in
  the intended-use document; it is not self-certified by the system owner.

## Scope and existing provisions
- Applies to all AI and machine-learning systems developed internally and to AI systems procured from the technology subsidiary or external vendors for which the Bank is responsible.
- Commits the Bank to lawful, fair, transparent, accountable AI operating under defined human oversight.
- Requires alignment with information security, personal data protection, risk management and IT governance policies.
- Reviewed annually by the AI Governance Committee and on material change.
