# PR-SEC-003 — Information Security and Cyber Incident Management Procedure

- **Procedure owner:** Information Security Directorate
- **Status:** Published - ISO 42001 addition pending
- **Related Annex A controls:** A.8.4
- **RAG corpus layer:** Layer 2 — internal governance

## Purpose
Defines detection, grading, response and reporting for information security and cyber incidents.

## Scope and existing provisions
- Incident classification, severity grading, response playbooks and notification duties.

> **ACTION REQUIRED - PENDING ADDITION**
>
> This procedure does not yet contain the ISO/IEC 42001 provisions set out below. The addition must be drafted, approved and published by **Information Security Directorate** as the procedure owner. Until it is published, AI systems assessed against A.8.4 may show gaps that system owners cannot remediate themselves.


## Required ISO 42001 additions (not yet published)
- AI-specific incident scenarios with severity grading and response playbooks: model poisoning, model inversion and extraction, training-data leakage, direct and indirect prompt injection, and jailbreak.
- Agentic scenarios: goal hijacking, tool misuse, identity abuse, memory poisoning, cascading failure and rogue agent.
- Who may declare an AI incident and the escalation route to the AI Governance Committee.

The provisions above have been proposed by the AI Governance Team. They are **not yet part of this procedure**. **Information Security Directorate** is responsible for drafting, approving and publishing them; the AI Governance Team tracks closure but does not author other directorates' procedures.
