# PR-AIGOV-004 — AI System Impact Assessment Procedure

- **Procedure owner:** AI Governance Team (Data Management and Analytics Directorate)
- **Status:** Published
- **Related Annex A controls:** A.5.2, A.5.3, A.5.4, A.5.5
- **RAG corpus layer:** Layer 2 — internal governance

## Purpose
Defines how the potential consequences of AI systems for individuals, groups of individuals and societies are identified, assessed and addressed.

## Scope and existing provisions
- Required for every AI system in scope of the management system; performed at planned intervals and when significant changes are proposed.
- Considers legal status and life opportunities, physical and psychological wellbeing, universal human rights and effects on societies.
- Results are documented and retained; the outcome informs the risk assessment and the Committee decision.

> **ACTION REQUIRED - PENDING ADDITION**
>
> This procedure does not yet contain the ISO/IEC 42001 provisions set out below. The addition must be drafted, approved and published by **AI Governance Team (Data Management and Analytics Directorate)** as the procedure owner. Until it is published, AI systems assessed against A.5.2, A.5.3, A.5.4, A.5.5 may show gaps that system owners cannot remediate themselves.


## Required ISO 42001 additions (not yet published)
- Integration with the personal-data impact assessment into a single instrument and a single record where personal data is processed.
- Explicit method for assessing societal impacts, including environmental, economic and access-to-services effects.

The provisions above have been proposed by the AI Governance Team. They are **not yet part of this procedure**. **AI Governance Team (Data Management and Analytics Directorate)** is responsible for drafting, approving and publishing them; the AI Governance Team tracks closure but does not author other directorates' procedures.
