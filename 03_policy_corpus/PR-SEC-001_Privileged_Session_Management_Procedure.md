# PR-SEC-001 — Privileged Session Management Procedure

- **Procedure owner:** Information Security Directorate
- **Status:** Published - ISO 42001 addition pending
- **Related Annex A controls:** A.3.2
- **RAG corpus layer:** Layer 2 — internal governance

## Purpose
Governs privileged access sessions and their monitoring.

## Scope and existing provisions
- Session recording, approval and time limits for privileged accounts.

> **ACTION REQUIRED - PENDING ADDITION**
>
> This procedure does not yet contain the ISO/IEC 42001 provisions set out below. The addition must be drafted, approved and published by **Information Security Directorate** as the procedure owner. Until it is published, AI systems assessed against A.3.2 may show gaps that system owners cannot remediate themselves.


## Required ISO 42001 additions (not yet published)
- Session management and permission boundaries for users who manage AI models or change their parameters.
- Service accounts and API keys used by model endpoints and agents brought into scope.
- Separation of duties between the person who trains a model and the person who approves its deployment.
- Identity and permission boundaries for autonomous agents acting on behalf of users.

The provisions above have been proposed by the AI Governance Team. They are **not yet part of this procedure**. **Information Security Directorate** is responsible for drafting, approving and publishing them; the AI Governance Team tracks closure but does not author other directorates' procedures.
