# PR-HR-001 — Training and Competence Procedure

- **Procedure owner:** Human Resources Directorate
- **Status:** Published - ISO 42001 addition pending
- **Related Annex A controls:** A.4.6
- **RAG corpus layer:** Layer 2 — internal governance

## Purpose
Defines competence requirements, training and awareness across the Bank.

## Scope and existing provisions
- Role-based competence definitions, training plans, records and effectiveness evaluation.

> **ACTION REQUIRED - PENDING ADDITION**
>
> This procedure does not yet contain the ISO/IEC 42001 provisions set out below. The addition must be drafted, approved and published by **Human Resources Directorate** as the procedure owner. Until it is published, AI systems assessed against A.4.6 may show gaps that system owners cannot remediate themselves.


## Required ISO 42001 additions (not yet published)
- AI competence requirements by role: developer, validator, business owner and oversight operator.
- Mandatory AI awareness training covering the AI policy, prohibited uses, automation bias and how to escalate a concern.
- Human oversight is only meaningful where the person exercising it has been trained for it.

The provisions above have been proposed by the AI Governance Team. They are **not yet part of this procedure**. **Human Resources Directorate** is responsible for drafting, approving and publishing them; the AI Governance Team tracks closure but does not author other directorates' procedures.
