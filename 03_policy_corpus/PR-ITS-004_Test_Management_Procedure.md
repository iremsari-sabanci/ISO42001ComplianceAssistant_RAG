# PR-ITS-004 — Test Management Procedure

- **Procedure owner:** IT Strategy and Governance Directorate
- **Status:** Published - ISO 42001 addition pending
- **Related Annex A controls:** A.7.4, A.7.6
- **RAG corpus layer:** Layer 2 — internal governance

## Purpose
Defines test planning, test data management and test execution.

## Scope and existing provisions
- Test levels, test data provisioning and masking of test data.

> **ACTION REQUIRED - PENDING ADDITION**
>
> This procedure does not yet contain the ISO/IEC 42001 provisions set out below. The addition must be drafted, approved and published by **IT Strategy and Governance Directorate** as the procedure owner. Until it is published, AI systems assessed against A.7.4, A.7.6 may show gaps that system owners cannot remediate themselves.


## Required ISO 42001 additions (not yet published)
- Training, validation and test datasets brought explicitly within the masking rules.
- Prohibition of test-set leakage into training data.
- Documented anonymisation or synthetic substitution where personal data is used for model training.
- Data-leakage testing: whether sensitive training values can be recovered from model outputs.

The provisions above have been proposed by the AI Governance Team. They are **not yet part of this procedure**. **IT Strategy and Governance Directorate** is responsible for drafting, approving and publishing them; the AI Governance Team tracks closure but does not author other directorates' procedures.
