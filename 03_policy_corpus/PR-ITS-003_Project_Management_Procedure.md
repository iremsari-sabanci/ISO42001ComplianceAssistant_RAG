# PR-ITS-003 — Project Management Procedure

- **Procedure owner:** IT Strategy and Governance Directorate
- **Status:** Published - ISO 42001 addition pending
- **Related Annex A controls:** A.5.2, A.5.3
- **RAG corpus layer:** Layer 2 — internal governance

## Purpose
Defines project governance, compliance and quality control activities.

## Scope and existing provisions
- Project lifecycle, compliance checks and quality control activities.

> **ACTION REQUIRED - PENDING ADDITION**
>
> This procedure does not yet contain the ISO/IEC 42001 provisions set out below. The addition must be drafted, approved and published by **IT Strategy and Governance Directorate** as the procedure owner. Until it is published, AI systems assessed against A.5.2, A.5.3 may show gaps that system owners cannot remediate themselves.


## Required ISO 42001 additions (not yet published)
- AI system impact assessment added to the compliance control step as a gate: the compliance check fails where an in-scope AI project has no completed impact assessment.
- A re-assessment trigger in the change section, since assessments are required at planned intervals and on significant change.

The provisions above have been proposed by the AI Governance Team. They are **not yet part of this procedure**. **IT Strategy and Governance Directorate** is responsible for drafting, approving and publishing them; the AI Governance Team tracks closure but does not author other directorates' procedures.
