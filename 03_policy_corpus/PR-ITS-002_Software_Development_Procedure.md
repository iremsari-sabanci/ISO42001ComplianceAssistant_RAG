# PR-ITS-002 — Software Development Procedure

- **Procedure owner:** IT Strategy and Governance Directorate
- **Status:** Published - ISO 42001 addition pending
- **Related Annex A controls:** A.6.1.3, A.6.2.2
- **RAG corpus layer:** Layer 2 — internal governance

## Purpose
Defines the software development lifecycle from requirement to deployment.

## Scope and existing provisions
- Stakeholder requirements, request evaluation, service level requirement records, service design package, design, coding, integration and test, deployment.

> **ACTION REQUIRED - PENDING ADDITION**
>
> This procedure does not yet contain the ISO/IEC 42001 provisions set out below. The addition must be drafted, approved and published by **IT Strategy and Governance Directorate** as the procedure owner. Until it is published, AI systems assessed against A.6.1.3, A.6.2.2 may show gaps that system owners cannot remediate themselves.


## Required ISO 42001 additions (not yet published)
- An AI flag at request intake in the project/demand management application, mandatory for AI requests, driving routing to the Analytics service and linking the request to the AI System Inventory record.
- AI-specific additions to the service design package: data provenance, data minimisation requirements and model performance and accuracy metrics.
- Deployment steps adjusted for model artefacts and model version control.

The provisions above have been proposed by the AI Governance Team. They are **not yet part of this procedure**. **IT Strategy and Governance Directorate** is responsible for drafting, approving and publishing them; the AI Governance Team tracks closure but does not author other directorates' procedures.
