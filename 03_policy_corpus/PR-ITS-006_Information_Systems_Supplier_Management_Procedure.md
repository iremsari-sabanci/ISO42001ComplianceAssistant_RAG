# PR-ITS-006 — Information Systems Supplier Management Procedure

- **Procedure owner:** IT Strategy and Governance Directorate
- **Status:** Published - ISO 42001 addition pending
- **Related Annex A controls:** A.10.2, A.10.3, A.10.4
- **RAG corpus layer:** Layer 2 — internal governance

## Purpose
Governs selection, contracting and monitoring of information systems suppliers.

## Scope and existing provisions
- Supplier assessment, contractual requirements, ongoing monitoring and exit.

> **ACTION REQUIRED - PENDING ADDITION**
>
> This procedure does not yet contain the ISO/IEC 42001 provisions set out below. The addition must be drafted, approved and published by **IT Strategy and Governance Directorate** as the procedure owner. Until it is published, AI systems assessed against A.10.2, A.10.3, A.10.4 may show gaps that system owners cannot remediate themselves.


## Required ISO 42001 additions (not yet published)
- An AI contract annex: explainability commitments; whether Bank data may be used to train the supplier's models; model performance warranties and degradation notification; notice of model version changes; right to audit or receive evidence; and AI-specific incident notification duties.
- Allocation of lifecycle responsibilities between the Bank, partners, suppliers and customers.

The provisions above have been proposed by the AI Governance Team. They are **not yet part of this procedure**. **IT Strategy and Governance Directorate** is responsible for drafting, approving and publishing them; the AI Governance Team tracks closure but does not author other directorates' procedures.
